How to read this

Weeks of stock: sales
Homes for sale divided by the average weekly sales over the last four weeks. A rising figure means stock is building faster than buyers are absorbing it.
Weeks of stock: rentals
Homes for rent divided by the average homes leased per week over the last four weeks. A rising figure means more choice for tenants and longer vacancies for landlords.
Why four-week averages
Weekly sales swing by 15% or more from one week to the next. Averaging four weeks shows the trend instead of the noise.
Where the numbers come from
REIWA's weekly Perth market snapshot. Sales are those reported by REIWA members, so they undercount the whole market but track its direction.

Source: Real Estate Institute of Western Australia (REIWA), Perth weekly market snapshots. Figures are REIWA's. Where a snapshot's body text disagreed with its headline (week ending 25/10/2026), the headline figure is used.

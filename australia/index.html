How to read this

Mean dwelling price
The total value of all homes in a state divided by the number of homes. It moves with the whole housing stock, not just the homes that sold.
Capital city median house and sales
The middle price, and the number, of established houses and units that changed hands in the capital city. The ABS revises the latest quarter up as late settlements arrive, so the newest sales count often understates the final figure.
New home loans
Loans committed to owner-occupiers and investors to buy or build a home. A leading sign of demand: loans are approved before purchases settle.
Dwellings approved and unemployment
Approvals show the supply coming in the next one to three years. Unemployment shows whether buyers keep their incomes, which decides whether a slowdown stays orderly.
